## English

- Fixed an Alibaba Cloud Audio3 timing issue that could add extra silence when audio arrived late, increasing subtitle delay.
- Improved Windows audio capture shutdown and restart reliability.

Update through the app or download the package for your platform. Quit portable Windows builds before replacing the executable. macOS packages remain self-signed and are not Apple-notarized. Android keeps its existing signing identity.

The reported long-session delay still needs a retest in the original Windows environment.

Thanks @yuxino.
