## English

- Intel builds running on Apple silicon can now update to the native Apple silicon package, including a same-version replacement after receiving this update.
- Improved xAI recognition recovery after temporary connection failures. Gemini and xAI configuration errors now keep their actionable message instead of retrying.
- A new session refreshes the subtitle confirmation time even when the text repeats. Complete custom-recognition settings can now repair a damaged saved entry while preserving independent translation settings.

Update through the app or download the package for your platform. Quit portable Windows builds before replacing the executable. macOS packages remain self-signed and are not Apple-notarized. Android keeps its existing signing identity.

Live-service recovery and latency, complete in-place or cross-architecture updates, physical Windows/Linux/Android devices, and long-running sessions remain unverified.
